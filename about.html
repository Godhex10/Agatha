What I believe

A home should feel like a deep breath at the end of a long day. It should hold your family's mess and your best dinner parties, your quiet Sunday mornings and your loudest celebrations — and look beautiful doing it.

My journey

From a Sketchbook to 350 Homes

  1. 2012

    Falling for interiors

    Graduated in interior design and spent a summer restoring the family farmhouse — the project that started it all.

  2. 2014

    Learning the craft

    Four years at a boutique studio designing family homes and small hotels, learning every trade on site.

  3. 2018

    The studio is born

    Agatha's Interior Design opens with one client, one sketchbook and a kitchen-table office.

  4. 2020

    Design goes online

    E-Design launches, so families anywhere can work with the studio from their own sofa.

  5. 2023

    350 happy homes

    A growing network of 40+ local makers, craftspeople and trusted trades.

  6. 2025

    A greener promise

    Low-VOC finishes and sustainably sourced pieces become standard on every project.
